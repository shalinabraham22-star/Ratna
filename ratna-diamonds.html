Started at a workbench in Kochi, in 2011

SA

Shalin Abraham

Founder & Chief Designer

Trained as a gemologist before she trained as a jeweller, Shalin still hand-checks every stone that enters the workshop before it's set.

RATNA began as a single workbench and a promise: no piece leaves the workshop until Shalin has looked at the stone herself.

Fifteen years later, that promise hasn't changed. We still cut our own settings in-house, still grade every diamond against GIA and IGI certificates, and still design each piece around how a woman actually moves through her day — not how it photographs on a stand.

Every RATNA piece carries a certificate, a lifetime cleaning service, and Shalin's own sign-off.
